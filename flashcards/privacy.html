This is a personal flashcard site run by Surya. You can study without an account. Signing in with Google is optional, and only lets your progress follow you across devices. The site also counts visits, as explained below, and you can turn that off.

Visit counting, even if you don't sign in

To see how many people use the site and come back, your browser creates a random visitor ID and saves it. Unless you turn this off, the site stores this with that ID:

  • When the ID was first and last seen, and the number of visits.
  • How many cards are marked as known in that browser.

It doesn't store your IP address or location, browser details, or which cards you look at.

The flashcards page shows the total number of views and unique users. These are site-wide totals, and don't show anything about any one person.

If you sign in, this record is linked to your account, including your earlier visits from that browser. Signing out starts a new ID, which isn't linked unless you sign in again.

To turn it off, use the "turn off" link at the bottom of the flashcards page. That also deletes this browser's record, and it applies to this browser only. It is also off if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

If you sign in, what is stored

  • Your account: your Google account ID, email address and name, as provided by Google.
  • Sign-in activity: your first and latest sign-in, the number of sign-ins, and when you last used the site.
  • Your preferences: your text size and last deck.
  • Your progress: which cards you marked, whether you know them, and when.

Where it is kept and who can see it

It is kept in a private Google Sheet owned by Surya, on Google's servers, and handled by a Google Apps Script. Surya can see everything in that sheet, including your name and email address. It is not public.

Your data is used only to run sign-in, sync your progress, and understand how the site is used. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing. The site has no ads and no third-party analytics.

What stays in your browser

Your progress, preferences and visitor ID are saved in your browser, whether or not you sign in. If you sign in, your browser also keeps a session token for up to 30 days, renewed as you use the site. Signing out removes your session, progress and visitor ID from that browser, but not from the sheet.

Google

  • Sign-in button: it loads from Google, so Google may receive information about your visit, even if you don't sign in.
  • Know more button: it opens a Google search containing the card's topic and description. Nothing about your account is added to that search by this site.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Your data, including linked visit records, is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. To ask, or with any question about your data, email suryarao.r@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with.

Please sign out on your devices first. A device that is still signed in can upload your progress again.
